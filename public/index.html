Know what you are training for

The 5-day SSB procedure

  1. Day 1

    Screening

    OIR test and the Picture Perception and Discussion Test (PPDT). Only screened-in candidates continue.

  2. Day 2

    Psychology

    TAT, WAT, SRT and Self Description. They show how you naturally think and react.

  3. Day 3 and 4

    GTO tasks

    Group discussion, group planning, outdoor tasks and the lecturette test your teamwork and leadership.

  4. Alongside

    Interview

    A personal interview with the Interviewing Officer, built on your PIQ form and your life so far.

  5. Day 5

    Conference

    All assessors meet, and results are announced.

Three ways to use it

Fits around your routine

NDA

The school student

Studies in the day and trains in the evening. Keeps a short timeline: a morning run, 20 OIR questions and one WAT round before bed.

CDS

Has long study hours and needs consistency. Uses the streak and the 5-week heatmap, and adds group discussion and lecturette practice.

Has only an hour or two a day. Keeps a few key tasks, watches the SSB countdown and checks the leaderboard for motivation.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is SSB Tracker free?

Yes. It is a student project, and creating an account costs nothing.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before they are saved, and every aspirant can only see their own tasks and scores. The leaderboard shows just a name, entry type, tasks done and best quiz score.

How is my rank decided?

Every task you complete adds to your total. As the total grows, you move up the ranks shown on your dashboard.

Can I change the daily tasks?

Yes. Add your own tasks with a time and a test area, and delete the ones you do not need.

Where is my progress saved?

In the SQLite database on the server, so your tasks, streak and quiz scores are still there the next time you log in.

Is this an official SSB website?

No. It is an independent student project and is not connected to the Services Selection Board or the Armed Forces.
