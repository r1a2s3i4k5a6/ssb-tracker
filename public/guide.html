The 5-day process

The Services Selection Board (SSB) tests your whole personality over five days. Nobody is judged on one task alone.

  1. Day 0

    Reporting

    Reach the centre, complete documents and get your chest number.

  2. Day 1

    Screening

    OIR test and PPDT. Candidates who clear the screening continue.

  3. Day 2

    Psychology

    TAT, WAT, SRT and SDT, one after the other.

  4. Days 3 and 4

    GTO tasks

    Group tasks, lecturette and individual obstacles. Personal interviews run alongside.

  5. Day 5

    Conference

    Each candidate meets the board, and the final result is announced.

Every test explained

What it is, and how to approach it. You can practise WAT, TAT, SRT and OIR in the practice room.

Screening

Day 1

OIR test

Verbal and non-verbal reasoning questions on series, analogies, codes, directions and arithmetic. Speed and accuracy both matter. Practise in the OIR quiz.

PPDT

A picture is shown for 30 seconds. You note the characters, mood and action, then write a story in 4 minutes. Later, each candidate narrates a story and the group agrees on one common story.

Psychology tests

Day 2

TAT

12 pictures are shown for 30 seconds each, with 4 minutes to write a story for each. The last slide is blank. Keep the hero positive, active and successful.

WAT

60 words appear for 15 seconds each. Write the first natural, positive sentence that comes to mind. Short and action-based sentences work best.

SRT

60 real-life situations in 30 minutes, which is about 30 seconds each. Write what you would actually do, quickly and practically.

SDT

About 15 minutes to describe what your parents, teachers and friends think of you, what you think of yourself, and the kind of person you want to become.

GTO tasks

Days 3 and 4

Group Discussion

The group talks over a given topic. Speak clearly, listen to others, and add new points instead of repeating.

Group Planning Exercise

A problem is shown on a model or map. You plan a solution alone, then the group agrees on one plan.

Progressive Group Task

The group crosses obstacles using planks, ropes and a load, with rules that get tougher at each stage.

Half Group Task

The same kind of task with half the group, so each candidate gets more time to show leadership.

Lecturette

You speak for about three minutes on a topic of your choice from a few given. Prepare a start, three main points and an end.

Individual Obstacles

You cross a series of obstacles on your own. It checks courage, stamina and willingness to try.

Command Task

You pick two helpers and lead them to cross obstacles. It tests planning, quick decisions and leading by example.

Final Group Task

One last group task that shows how well the team works together after several days.

Interview and Conference

Alongside, then Day 5

Personal Interview

A 30 to 60 minute talk with the Interviewing Officer, based on your Personal Information Questionnaire (PIQ): family, studies, hobbies, current affairs and why you want to join the forces. Be honest and calm.

Conference

All assessors meet each candidate in turn, discuss the full picture and announce the result. Stay relaxed and answer simply.

The 15 Officer Like Qualities (OLQs)

Assessors look for these qualities in every test. Your dashboard shows one OLQ to practise each day.

Do and don't

Do

  • Be natural and honest in every test
  • Keep stories and sentences positive and active
  • Listen carefully in group tasks
  • Stay physically fit with daily exercise
  • Read a newspaper every day
  • Be polite and cheerful with everyone
  • Sleep well and eat properly before the test
  • Admit it when you do not know something

Don't

  • Do not memorise answers or copy ideas
  • Do not dominate or interrupt in group tasks
  • Do not pretend to be someone you are not
  • Do not give negative or sad endings to stories
  • Do not argue with the assessors
  • Do not panic after one bad task
  • Do not neglect your fitness until the last week
  • Do not leave the PIQ unrevised

A simple 30-day plan

Use this with the daily timeline on your dashboard.

Week 1

Build the base

Fix a daily routine, read one editorial every day, practise 20 OIR questions daily and start a morning run.

Week 2

Psychology tests

Write 20 WAT sentences and 10 SRT reactions daily, one TAT story, and a first draft of your SDT.

Week 3

GTO and speaking

Hold a group discussion with friends three times a week, practise lecturettes and play team sports.

Week 4

Interview and mocks

Revise your PIQ aloud, do a mock interview, then a full mock of every test. Rest well before the board.

What to use

Daily newspaper

Read editorials and note three facts each day for current affairs.

Reasoning workbook

Any standard verbal and non-verbal reasoning book for extra OIR practice.

Practice with friends

Group discussions, mock interviews and lecturettes are best done with other people.
