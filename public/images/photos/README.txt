TIPS
- Use .jpg and keep each file under about 400 KB so the page stays fast (squoosh.app can shrink them).
- A dark or evenly lit photo works best, because the site has a dark theme and the text sits on top of it.
- Use free photos only (Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay) and mention them in your report.
